Privacy Policy

How C2C handles your saved station preference and information.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the C2C Alexa skill handles information when you use it to request live train information.

The skill is operated by an independent individual developer. For privacy questions or requests, contact notcrypticblade@gmail.com.

1. Information we store

When you set a preferred origin station, the skill stores the station preference in a cloud-hosted database associated with your Alexa userId.

The userId is an identifier supplied by Alexa that allows the skill to associate requests with the same enabled Alexa account. It is not, by itself, your name or email address, but it is treated as information that can be linked to your use of the skill.

2. Why we use this information

We use your saved origin station to:

  • Remember your preferred starting station between requests.
  • Let you request train information using fewer words.
  • Retrieve relevant train information for your journey.

We do not need your name, home address or email address to provide this saved-station feature.

3. Where information is stored

Your preferred origin station and its associated Alexa user identifier are stored in a cloud database used by the skill's backend. The database service provider and its applicable privacy and security arrangements depend on the hosting configuration.

Alexa also processes information when you interact with the skill. Amazon's handling of Alexa voice requests and account information is governed by Amazon's own privacy information.

Read Amazon's Alexa privacy information .

4. Live train information

The skill retrieves train information from C2C-related data sources to respond to your requests. The saved origin station is used to simplify those requests.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain your saved origin station and its associated Alexa user identifier until you ask the skill to forget or delete the saved preference. When a deletion request is processed, the associated saved preference will be removed from the active database.

To remove your saved station, use the skill's voice-based forget or reset command, if available. If that command does not work, email notcrypticblade@gmail.com and request deletion of your saved C2C station preference.

Disabling the skill in Alexa may change the Alexa user identifier supplied to the skill in the future; do not assume that disabling the skill automatically deletes an existing database record.

6. Sharing and disclosure

The saved preference is used to provide the skill's functionality. The backend and cloud database providers may process information as necessary to operate and secure the service. Train data providers may receive queries needed to retrieve train information.

We do not sell your saved station preference or associated Alexa user identifier.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect the database and restrict access to stored preferences. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. Contact us using the email address above to make a request.

9. Children's privacy

This skill is intended to provide train information and is not specifically designed to collect children's personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the skill's functionality, data handling or legal obligations change. The latest version will be available at this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests or deletion requests, email notcrypticblade@gmail.com.
